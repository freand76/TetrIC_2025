                                         The TetrIC project was created by Fredrik Andersson, Torbjörn Aronsson, Mats Christianson, Mathias Johansson, Jan-Peter Nilsson, and Mattias Severson. The ASIC was developed at the Lund Institute of Technology in the Department of Electroscience, with extensive assistance from Anders Berkeman and Martin Nilsson. The ASIC project required approximately 4,000 man-hours to complete and is integrated onto 25 square millimeters of silicon. The TetrIC game was programmed by Fredrik Andersson. Remarkably, 25 years later, the project was re-implemented on a Tang Nano FPGA.
